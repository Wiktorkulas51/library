# Legal Pages Infrastructure Plan

Automate legal pages to accelerate project startup.

## Objectives
- Automated generation of Privacy Policy and Cookies pages.
- Centralized company metadata.
- Reusable `LegalLayout`.

## Proposed Changes
1.  **Data**: `src/data/company.json`.
2.  **Content**: `src/content/legal/privacy-policy.json`, `src/content/legal/cookies.json`.
3.  **Layout**: `src/layouts/LegalLayout.astro`.
4.  **Pages**: `src/pages/polityka-prywatnosci.astro`, `src/pages/cookies.astro`.
